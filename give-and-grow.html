How it works

Grow With Gratus! Give And Grow Together!

Give and grow

Five minutes, start to finish. There is no account, nothing to install, and nothing to pay before you have seen whether you like it.

1 · Write one true thing

Open the app and go to Grow. The question is on the screen and the box is under it. Write one thing you are grateful for. It does not have to be large. The bus was on time is a real day.

2 · Give it an emoji

The emoji is the plant. Everything you write with it from now on feeds the same thing. Most people settle on two or three they keep coming back to: a heart for a person, a cup for a morning, a leaf for being outside.

3 · Come again tomorrow

A day counts when you write and choose an emoji. One entry, one day, for that plant. Writing twice in a day does not count twice, and missing a day takes nothing away.

The five phases

Planted at day 0 · Nurtured at 2 · Deepened at 5 · Bloomed at 9 · Ready to give at 13. Crossing into a phase is the only time the app makes a sound: five phases are five notes of one chord.

4 · Give it away

Open Give, choose what grew, say who it is for and write why. You get a link. The whole gift lives inside the link: the emoji, the days it took, your words, and a short journey that opens when they tap it. Send it however you like.

When they plant it, the days come with it. A gift of twenty days starts at twenty in their garden, not at zero, and keeps growing from there. That is the part people do not expect.

What people use it for

A birthday that is not a thing

Start a plant a month before. Write one line a day about them. Give it on the day: thirty days of having thought about somebody, which no shop sells.

A team, after a hard quarter

One person grows a plant for the team and writes a line each day about something that went right. At thirteen days it is ready. Give it in the retro, instead of a slide.

Grief, at your own speed

A plant for somebody you lost. One line when you can, nothing when you cannot. Nothing decays, nothing nags, and the garden holds the days you managed.

A practice you keep failing

No streak means no failure state. People who bounced off every other journal stay here because there is nothing to break and nothing to restart.

Giving to a project, with words

Give on Giveth in your own wallet, then plant a Gratus Seed: why you gave. The project reads it and writes back. A donation is a number; a seed is a person.

A room of a few people

A Vibe is a small group with a code. No accounts, no feed to perform for. A handful of people saying out loud what they are grateful for.
