## How we evolve this
- Every threshold line, statement, recipe why, lineage reason, and Grace line is in `config/` and flagged `owner: true` — reword freely; the gates will keep the forbidden words out.
- `config/growth.json` and `config/rewards.json` hold every number the spec says the owner tunes. Nothing is hard-coded elsewhere.
- New family? Add it to `families.json` and a `morph` case in `flora.js`. New form? Add a row in `growth.json` and a branch in `renderPlant`. The engine tests will tell you what else moved.
