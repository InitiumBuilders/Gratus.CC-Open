The story

Give And Grow What Matters Most

Gratus.CC is a gratitude journal that grows a garden, and a way to give a gift made of days. It began with one sentence, and the sentence is still the whole product.

Gratus means honor.

Everything here follows from that.

What it is

You write one true thing you are grateful for. You give it an emoji. That emoji becomes a plant, and every day you write with it, the plant grows. After thirteen days it is ready to give, and when you give it, the days go with it into somebody else's garden.

That is the whole mechanic. A gift here is not made of money and not made of a moment. It is made of days. Nobody can shortcut days, buy them, or fake them. The only way to have thirteen days of attention to give away is to have paid attention for thirteen days.

Gratus Gives Gifts That Keep On Growing

A gift arrives carrying the days it took, and keeps counting from there.

Why it exists

Almost every app that touches gratitude turns it into a scoreboard. A run of days that punishes you for a hard week. Points, badges, a number that falls the moment you stop performing. That is a machine for producing guilt, wearing the clothes of a practice.

Gratus has no streaks, no scores and no ranks, and it never will. Nothing here decays. There is nothing to break and nobody to be compared to. A day you gave is a day you keep, forever. Come back after a year and the garden is exactly as you left it, waiting.

What it refuses

  • No scores, no ranks, no leaderboards, ever. The one reading in the whole app is shown to a project about itself, and it orders nobody against anybody.
  • Your journal never leaves your device. No account is required, no copy is kept on a server, and nothing you write is read by anybody, including us.
  • No invented numbers. Every figure in this app is one it counted. A gate refuses a build that prints one it did not.
  • No words in anybody's mouth. No fabricated quotes, no ratings nobody gave.
