**A photo.** Tap Photo and choose one. It is scaled down on your device before it is saved
and it never leaves your device. It lives in the entry.

**Your voice.** Tap Voice and speak. The recording is sent once, transcribed, and the text
comes back into the box for you to edit. The audio itself is kept on your device, not on a
server.

**The allowance.** Transcription costs real money on a real account, so there is a ceiling:
forty recordings per device per day and a larger one across everybody. If you reach it, the
app says so plainly and you can still type.

**If voice is not offered**, the key is not configured on that deployment. Nothing is
broken; the feature is simply off.

**Privacy.** Only the audio for a recording you made goes out, only at the moment you make
it, and only to be turned into text.
