The laws are the sentences this product is built to obey. You can read all twelve in the
app, from the menu.

The first is **Gratus means honor.** Everything else follows from it.

They cover what this is for and what it refuses to be. A few of the ones with the most
consequence in the code:

- **Nothing decays.** No streak, no decay, no penalty for being away. Days are kept.
- **Nobody is ranked.** No scores, no leaderboards, no comparison between people. The one
  reading in the app is shown to a project about itself.
- **Words belong to whoever wrote them.** A seed is held until its author publishes it, and
  a journal entry never leaves the device.
- **A gift is made of days.** Not of money, and not of a moment. The days are the gift.

A gate reads the list of words this product does not say and refuses a build that says one.
