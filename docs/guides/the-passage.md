The Passage is a page you can share that shows what you are growing without showing what
you wrote.

Open it from the menu, or go to /passage.

**What it shows.** Your emojis, their phases, the days of care. The shape of the thing.

**What it never shows.** The words. Entries stay on your device; the Passage carries the
garden, not the journal.

**A code.** A Passage carries a short code. Somebody with the code sees your page. Somebody
without it does not.

**Why it exists.** Gratitude is easier to keep up when somebody else can see that you are
keeping it up. This is the version of that which does not require handing over your journal.
