Watering is writing back to somebody who gave to you.

**Open** your console with your project key. You see every seed, including the ones whose
authors have not published them.

**Water one.** A few sentences. Twenty seconds. It lands in their garden as a bloom and it
stays inside the seed forever.

**Passing it on.** You can name a project you are grateful for. The person who gave to you
sees it and can carry the flow onward.

**The reading.** Your console shows how many seeds you have, how many you answered and how
long you took. It is shown to you first and it orders nobody against anybody.

**Why it matters.** A donation receipt is a number. A reply is a person. The people who
gave to you wrote to you by hand; this is the other half of that.
