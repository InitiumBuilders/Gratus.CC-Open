Every growing thing moves through five phases, by days:

| phase | at |
|---|---|
| Planted | day 0 |
| Nurtured | day 2 |
| Deepened | day 5 |
| Bloomed | day 9 |
| Ready to give | day 13 |

Crossing into a phase is the only time the app makes a sound: five phases are five notes of
one chord, and each crossing plays the chord as far as you have come. The fifth one falls
from the top and leaves the root ringing, which is what giving sounds like.

**Ready to give is not finished.** A plant can stay in your garden at thirty days or three
hundred. Ready means only that it now carries enough to be worth handing to somebody.

You can give something earlier than thirteen days. The app will let you. It carries fewer
days, and it is still a gift.
