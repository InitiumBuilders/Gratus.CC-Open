A Vibe is a small group with a code. No accounts: you hold the code, you are in the room.

**Starting one.** Open Vibes, Start a Vibe. Name it, say what it is for, pick a mark. You
get a code and a keeper key. The code lets anyone in; the keeper key can take a post down.
The keeper key is shown once.

**Joining.** Type the code. That is all.

**Posting.** A short gratitude and an emoji from your own garden. Your journal does not go
there; a room post is its own thing, written for the room.

**What travels.** What you post and the name you sign it with. Nothing else.

**Limits.** A room keeps its posts. When it fills up the older ones move to a page of their
own and are kept, because nothing here is deleted to make room.
