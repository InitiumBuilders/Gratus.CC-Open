The stream is one shared list of what people are growing toward. One line each.

**Where.** On the home screen, open Gratus Gives Together.

**Posting.** Write the line and post it. It carries the name on your sheet and the emoji of
something you are growing. It appears for everybody.

**It is public.** The app says so before you post. Write what you would be happy for a
stranger to read.

**A line cannot be taken back.** Nothing ties a line to you, an account included, so there
is no way to prove a line is yours and remove it from the stream. Think of it as saying something
out loud in a room.

**Limits.** Eight lines a day from one person, so that one voice cannot fill the room. When
the feed fills up the older lines move to a page of their own and are kept.
