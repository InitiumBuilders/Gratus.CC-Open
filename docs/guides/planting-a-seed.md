A seed is a few words written to a project you gave to.

**Where.** After you give on Giveth, the app is waiting with plant this Gratus.

**What to write.** Why you gave. Two sentences is plenty. It is read by a person.

**Who reads it.** The project's steward, once they have claimed the project. The app tells
you this before you write.

**Held or published.** Your words are held by default: the project sees them, nobody else
does. You can choose to publish them later, and then they appear on the project's public
Gratus page. That choice is yours and it is reversible.

**Your key.** Planting a seed gives you a key, kept on your device. It is what lets you
speak on that seed later and what proves the seed is yours.

**What happens next.** When the project writes back, the seed in your garden blooms.
