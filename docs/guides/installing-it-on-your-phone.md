Gratus installs like an app and then opens like one, without a store.

**iPhone.** Open gratus.cc in Safari. Share, then Add to Home Screen.

**Android.** Open it in Chrome. The menu offers Install app, or Add to Home screen.

**What you get.** Its own icon, no browser bars, and it opens offline. The shell is kept on
the device, so the app starts whether or not you have a signal.

**What needs a signal.** Voice transcription, the Giveth pages, rooms, the stream and seeds.
Writing, your garden, your journal and your glyph all work with no connection at all.

**Updating.** It updates itself when you open it with a signal. There is nothing to press.

**Your garden is per browser.** Installing does not move an existing garden from another
browser; export and restore does.
