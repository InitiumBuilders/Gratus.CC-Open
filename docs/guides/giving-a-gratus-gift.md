Open Give, then Give a Gratus Gift.

**Choose what grew.** The row shows your plants, longest-grown first. What you choose
carries its days.

**Say who it is for**, write why, and sign it.

**Wrap it.** You get a link. The whole gift lives inside the link: the emoji, the days, your
words, and the journey that opens when they tap it. There is no account on either end and
nothing is stored on a server.

**Send it however you like.** A message, an email, written on paper.

**What they see.** A short journey: the emoji arriving, the days it took, then your words.
At the end they can plant it in their own garden, and the days come with it.

**They can tell you it landed.** If they choose to, twice: that it arrived, and later that
it grew. See the guide on the Return.
