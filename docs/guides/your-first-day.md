Open Grow. The question is already on the screen and the box is under it.

Write one true thing. It does not have to be large. "The bus was on time" is a real day.

Choose an emoji. That emoji is the plant: everything you write with it from now on feeds
the same thing. Most people find two or three they keep coming back to.

Tap Plant My Gratus. That is day one.

**What just happened.** A plant appeared in your garden at the Planted phase. Nothing else
was asked of you and nothing was sent anywhere. Tomorrow, if you write with the same emoji,
it reaches Nurtured.

**If you want to say it out loud**, use Voice instead of Text. The recording is transcribed
on the way in and the recording itself stays on your device.
