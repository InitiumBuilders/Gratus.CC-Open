Your garden is every plant you have grown, laid out as a field of orbs. Open it from the
Gratus door.

**The field.** Each orb is one plant. Its size and light come from its phase, and gold
means ready to give. Tap one to look closer. Hold one for the three things you usually
want: look closer, write today with it, or give this one.

**Filters.** Everything, Growing, Ready to give. Sort by most days, newest, or name.

**Field or list.** The field is the picture; the list is the ledger. Both hold the same
plants.

**Families.** Every emoji belongs to one of eight families, each with its own species name
and its own line. You will see it on the sheet a hold opens.

**Recipes.** Some plants grown together long enough make a third thing. The Growth Book has
the ones that exist and lets you write your own.
