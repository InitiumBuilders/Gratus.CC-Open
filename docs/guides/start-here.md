Gratus is a gratitude journal that grows a garden, and a way to give a gift made of days.

You write one thing you are grateful for. You give it an emoji. That emoji becomes a plant,
and every day you write with it, the plant grows. After thirteen days it is ready to give,
and when you give it, the days go with it into somebody else's garden.

There is no account and nothing to sign up for. Your writing lives on your device. Open the
app and start; that is the whole beginning.

**Three doors.** Give is where gifts are made and received. Gratus is the middle, where
your garden and your journal live. Grow is where you write today. Swipe between them or tap
the bar at the bottom.

**Nothing here decays.** No streaks, no scores, no ranks. A day you gave is a day you keep.
If you are away for a month, nothing is taken from you in the meantime.
