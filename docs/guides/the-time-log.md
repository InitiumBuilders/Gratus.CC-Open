The Time Log shows when you wrote and how long each entry took.

**Where.** Open the Gratitude Journal and choose Time Log. It is also in the menu.

**The three numbers.** Days Kept is how many different days hold an entry. Entries is how
many you have written. Time Writing is the sum of the time each entry took.

**The calendar.** One month at a time. A lit day is a day you wrote. Tap one to read what
you wrote that day. The arrows move between months.

**The rows.** Under the calendar is every entry of that month: the day, the hour it was
written, its emoji, how long it took and how many words it has. Tap a row to open the entry.

**How the time is counted.** The clock runs while you are writing. A pause longer than a
minute and a half is left out, so time away from the page is not counted.

**Where it is kept.** On your device, with the entries themselves. Entries written before
the log existed show their day and no length.
