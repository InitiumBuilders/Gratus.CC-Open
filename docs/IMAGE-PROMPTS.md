# Gratus.CC · image and video prompts

Seven images, two videos. Each prompt names the subject, the material, the light, the palette, the ground, the frame, and ends with the same three words. Generate at 9:16 for the app scenes unless a prompt says otherwise; the app feathers every edge, so keep the subject in the centre 70% and let the corners fall dark.

Palette for all of them: deep space ground `#050912`, mint light `#6ED9C0`, leaf green `#7CF26A`, warm gold `#F2C97D`, star white `#FFF7D6`, a whisper of violet `#8B7CFF`.

## 1 · The Gratus emblem, risen (the app's own scene)
The Gratus star-leaf emblem: a four-pointed white-gold star with four translucent green glass leaves curving from its points, floating twenty metres above a still dark lake at night, the Milky Way behind it. The star is the only light source; its reflection is a thin gold line on the water. Glass leaves catch teal and lime; a few gold motes drift. Cinematic, photoreal, 9:16, subject centred and small, corners black. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## 2 · The first sprout (Grow)
A single luminous sprout with two leaves, made of pale green light, growing from a small mound of dark moss on a floating island the size of a table, at night, a crescent moon behind. Soft rim light in mint, a gold glow at the root where the sprout meets the soil, stars above, dark water below. Shallow depth of field, photoreal, 9:16, centred, corners dark. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## 3 · The heart that grew (Give)
A glowing red-gold heart made of woven light inside a clear crystal sphere, small green sprouts growing at its base inside the sphere, a thin ribbon of gold light tied in a bow at the top. The sphere rests on dark moss above Earth's curved horizon at night, city lights far below. Warm light from the heart, cool teal from the horizon. Photoreal, 9:16, centred, corners dark. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## 4 · The five phases (the Growth Book)
Five glass orbs on a slow spiral of gold light over still black water: the first holds a seed, the second a drop of water, the third a green shoot, the fourth an open flower of light, the fifth a small gold gift box. Each orb slightly larger than the last. Ripples under each one. Dark forest edges, out of focus. Photoreal, 9:16, spiral centred, corners dark. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## 5 · The Gratus Galaxy
A spiral galaxy seen from above, its arms made of thousands of tiny glowing leaves and stars in mint, green and gold, with a warm white-gold core. Four small brighter stars sit on the arms at the four compass points. Deep space around it, Earth's night horizon just visible at the bottom edge. 16:9 for the web and 9:16 for the app. Galaxy centred, corners black. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## 6 · The wish for tomorrow (the Vault)
A thin crescent ring of white-gold light standing upright on the surface of a still dark lake at night, a single vertical beam of light falling from the sky through its centre into the water, soft ripples spreading. Mountains far away, a few stars, mist low on the water. Quiet and vast. Photoreal, 9:16, ring centred, corners dark. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## 7 · The golden river (Earth, and the gift's journey)
A river of gold light winding from the bottom of the frame through a dark forested valley toward a sunrise on the far horizon, seen from high above. The river is the only warm light; the forest is deep teal-green; the sky turns from indigo to a thin band of gold. Photoreal, 9:16 for the app and 16:9 for the web, river centred, corners dark. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## Videos (15 seconds, loop, no sound)
- **The breathing star.** The emblem from prompt 1 alone on black, its glow slowly breathing, gold motes drifting upward, a very slow rotation of the leaves. Loops with no visible seam. 9:16 and 1:1.
- **The ripple.** A single drop of gold light falls into still black water and the rings spread outward and fade, then another drop. Camera still, top-down at a slight angle, dark forest edges. Loops with no visible seam. 9:16.
