# What leaves the device

**Your journal never leaves.** Entries, photos and recordings are held in your browser's
own storage, on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no copy on a server.

Four things travel, and each one only because you asked it to:

- **A gift.** The whole journey lives inside its link. You choose who gets the link.
- **A seed.** Words you write to a project you gave to. The app tells you before you write
  one that its steward will read it, and your words stay held until you publish them.
- **A goal.** One line on a shared stream. Everyone can see it, and the app says so.
- **A word in a room.** A Vibe is a small group with a code. What you say there is read by
  whoever holds the code.

Clearing your browser's storage for this site erases the garden. Export from the You sheet
if you want a copy you keep.
