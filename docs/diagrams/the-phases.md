# Five phases

A day counts when you write something and give it an emoji. The phases are:

| phase | at |
|---|---|
| Planted | day 0 |
| Nurtured | 2 |
| Deepened | 5 |
| Bloomed | 9 |
| Ready to give | 13 |

**Nothing decays.** A day you gave is a day you keep, and a plant never falls back to an
earlier phase because you missed a week. There are no streaks here and nothing is ever
taken away for being absent.

A gift carries its days with it. When somebody gives you a Gratus, the days it took come
into your garden with it, and they keep counting from there.
