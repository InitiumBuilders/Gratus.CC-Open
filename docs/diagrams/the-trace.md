# The Emotional Trace

Giveth's trace follows the money. This one follows the human energy behind it.

1. **Begin.** A donor gives on Giveth in their own wallet, then plants a Gratus Seed here:
   a short note, the project, and the transaction.
2. **Become.** The project's steward sees the seeds arrive beside the capital.
3. **Bridge.** The steward waters a seed with a short reply, and may pass the flow on to a
   project they are grateful for.
4. **Bloom.** The seed in the donor's garden opens into the project's own emoji and keeps
   the reply inside it.
5. **Answer.** Either side may keep speaking. A seed carries a thread, not a receipt.

A seed is written to be read by that project's steward, and the app says so before anyone
writes one. An open read returns only the seeds whose authors chose to publish them.
