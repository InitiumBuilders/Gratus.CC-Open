# Gratus.CC · the pitch

**Grow Gratus Give**

> Gratus means honor.

## The problem

Gratitude works and does not stick. People start a gratitude journal, feel the lift, and stop within weeks. The journal is a list; the list never changes; nothing in it points back at the person or out toward anyone else. Every gratitude app on the store is a text box with a a day in a row counter.

## The idea

Gratitude that grows, and can be given.

In Gratus, every line you write plants or feeds an emoji. The emoji grows a phase for every day you return to it: Planted, Nurtured, Deepened, Bloomed, Ready to Give. Two emojis that share enough days make a recipe. Your garden is the shape of your attention over time.

When something is grown, you can give it. A Gratus Gift is not a screenshot. It carries its whole journey in the link: the days, the message, the hands that held it. The person who opens it walks the journey and chooses to keep it, and then it keeps growing in their garden. Gratitude becomes a thing that moves between people.

## Why it is built this way

- **The journal stays on your phone.** No accounts, no server holds your words. The only things that leave are what you give and the goals you choose to post.
- **Nothing to buy.** There are no tiers, no ranks and no money words anywhere in the app; a gate refuses them at ship time.
- **The scene is the screen.** Every page stands on one of his paintings or looping videos, filling the whole screen. The app opens through the same ritual every time: the doorway, the light, the held white, then the garden.
- **Everything reads.** All ink is white and solid, nothing under 14px, every word on a dark plate or a scrim. Computed contrast is checked by a gate before a ship.
- **Simple is sacred.** One loop: write, plant, return, give. Everything else is a room off that loop.

## Who it is for

People who already want a gratitude practice and have lost one. People who want to give something that is not bought. Families and friends who would keep a garden together. Later, communities and projects that want gratitude to flow toward real work in the world.

## What exists now

The full front end, live at gratus.cc and installable: the three tabs, the garden, the Growth Book with journal, phases, emojis and sixty recipes, the complete gift journey, Gratus Goals with a shared stream, the twelve laws, the ritual, the scenes, the song. One small server function for the stream. The rest of Give (projects, Earth, the Vault, the World) is drawn and labelled "as imagined".
