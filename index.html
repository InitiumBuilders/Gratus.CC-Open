On your phone

Add Gratus to your home screen.

On Android, in Chrome, open the menu and choose “Add to Home screen”. On iPhone, in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Gratus opens full screen and works offline. There are no accounts and nothing to buy. Your journal stays on your device.
