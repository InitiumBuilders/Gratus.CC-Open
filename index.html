Grow

Plant today. A brighter tomorrow.

What are you grateful for today? Write it, tag it, and plant an emoji that grows a phase for every day you return to it. Post the Gratus Goals you are growing toward, and see everyone's in the stream.

Gratus

Your Gratitude Garden

Moments take root. Gratitude grows. A kinder world blooms. Every emoji you keep sits in your garden, and the Growth Book is your journal by day, with the phases, the emojis and the recipes.

Give

Turn gratitude into impact.

Give a Gratus Gift that carries its whole journey, support a project, or send a wish to tomorrow. Give what grows.

The first gift journey

Every gift carries a story.

Someone grows an emoji with days of gratitude and wraps it with a message. You unwrap it, walk its journey, see how it grew, and choose how to keep it. Then it can keep growing in your garden.

Add Gratus to your home screen.

Open the app on your phone. On Android, in Chrome, open the menu and choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”. On iPhone, in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Gratus opens full screen and works offline.
