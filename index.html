On your phone

Add Gratus to your home screen.

On Android, in Chrome, open the menu and choose “Add to Home screen”. On iPhone, in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Gratus opens full screen and works offline, and your journal stays on your device either way.

Opening a gift somebody sends you is free, for ever, with no account. Growing your own garden is free for a week, and $24 a month after that. An account is optional: it keeps a sealed copy of your garden that nobody here can read, and gives you a page at gratus.cc/gg/your-name.
