# Code of conduct

This is a project about gratitude. Behave accordingly.

## What is expected

Be kind. Assume the other person is trying. Ask before you assume. Disagree about the work
without making it about the person doing it.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Sexual language or imagery. Publishing somebody's
private information. Sustained disruption of a discussion. Anything that would make a
reasonable person not want to be here.

## If something goes wrong

Report it through GitHub's private reporting on this repository. Reports are read by the
maintainer, kept private, and answered.

Anyone who breaks this can have their contribution removed and their participation ended.
That decision belongs to the maintainer and is not negotiated in public.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere somebody is
representing the project.
