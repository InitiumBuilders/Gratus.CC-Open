What Gratus is

Gratus.CC is a gratitude journal with an emoji garden and gifts you can share. It is offered as is, free, while it is being built. Features may change or go away.

Your words

Everything you write is yours. It stays on your device unless you put it in a gift and share the link. Do not put in a gift anything you would not want the person, or anyone they forward the link to, to read.

Giving to projects

Project and partner cards are shown as imagined while the app is being built. Any giving happens on the partner's own site, under the partner's terms. Gratus holds no money and moves no money.

Care

Gratus is not a medical or mental health service. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services or a helpline in your country.

Changes

These terms change when the app does. The date at the top tells you when.
