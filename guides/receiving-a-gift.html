Receiving a gift

Somebody sent you a link. Open it.

The journey. The emoji arrives, then the days it took, then what they wrote. Take your time; nothing is timed and nothing expires.

Planting it. At the end you can put it in your garden. It arrives with every day it already carried, so a gift of twenty days starts at twenty, not at zero. From there it keeps growing with you.

You do not need an account. The gift is inside the link. If you never open the app again, the gift is still yours to read.

Telling them. You can send back that it landed, and later that it grew. Each is one tap and carries no words: they see the emoji, the phase and the day, and nothing else. It is your choice each time and you can do neither.
