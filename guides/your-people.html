Your people, and handing them a room

Keeping somebody puts their Gratus page on your list. It is one-sided on purpose: there is no request to approve, nothing for anyone to decline, and keeping somebody sends them nothing at all.

Keeping somebody. Open your account, then Your people. Type their handle and press Keep. A handle is the last part of somebody's page address, so the person at gratus.cc/gg/ben has the handle ben. Capitals are fine, because a handle is one case underneath. You can also open somebody's page while you are signed in on that device and press Keep close.

Together. If they keep you as well, both lists say together. Nothing else changes. Neither of you is told when the other keeps or lets go, because there is nothing here that needs announcing.

What a list carries. Only what their page already shows in public: how many plants are growing, how many days of care those plants hold, and the marks they chose to publish. Never a word from inside anybody's journal, and never the address behind an account.

Only a public page can be kept. If somebody has not made their page public there is nothing there to keep, and Gratus says so plainly. If they make it private later, your list says they have gone and shows nothing else about them.

Handing somebody a room. A Gratus Vibe is its code: whoever holds the code is in the room. From Your people, press "a room" beside somebody and choose which one. From inside a room, press "Bring somebody in" and your list is right there, one tap each. Either way Gratus writes the line and hands it to your phone to send. Gratus messages nobody for you, and it never has your friends' addresses to message them with.

Letting go. Press "let go" beside them. They leave your list, and their own list is untouched, because it was never yours to change.

Where it lives. A list belongs to an account, so it travels with you to a new phone the same way a saved garden does. Your journal stays where it always was, on the device you wrote it on.
