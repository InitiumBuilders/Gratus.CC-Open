Giving with Giveth

Giveth runs the money rail: zero-fee donations to verified projects, in your own wallet. Gratus adds the human rail on top of it.

What Gratus does. Reads. It never holds a key, never asks for a wallet and never touches a donation. The giving happens on giveth.io.

How it goes. Find a project in the Giveth door. Grow something for them if you like. When you are ready, Give on Giveth opens their page in your own wallet. Then plant a Gratus Seed: your words, paired with the project and the transaction.

The seed and the money, linked. Gratus asks Giveth whether that transaction really arrived, and writes down what Giveth says, not what anyone tells it.

Why bother. A donation is a number in a ledger. A seed is a person saying why. The project gets both.
