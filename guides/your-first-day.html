Your first day

The first time you open Gratus it offers to show you how, in three lit steps. Take them or skip them.

Open Grow, or tap Write Today. The question is on the screen and the box is under it.

Write one true thing. It can be small. "The bus was on time" is a whole day.

Choose an emoji. That emoji is the plant: everything you write with it from now on feeds the same one.

Tap Plant My Gratus. That is day one.

What just happened. A plant appeared in your garden at the Planted phase, and the journal has its first day. Nothing was sent anywhere. Write with the same emoji on a second day and it reaches Nurtured.

If you want to say it out loud, use Voice. Your voice is sent once to be turned into words, and the recording stays on your device.
