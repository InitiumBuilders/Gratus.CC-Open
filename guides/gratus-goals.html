Gratus Goals: the stream

The stream is one shared list of what people are growing toward. One line each.

Posting. Write the line, sign it with any name, choose an emoji. It appears for everybody.

It is public. The app says so before you post. Write what you would be happy for a stranger to read.

No accounts, so no deleting. There is nothing tying a line to you, which also means there is no way to prove a line is yours and take it back. Think of it as saying something out loud in a room.

Limits. Eight lines a day from one person, so that one voice cannot fill the room. When the feed fills up the older lines move to a page of their own and are kept.
