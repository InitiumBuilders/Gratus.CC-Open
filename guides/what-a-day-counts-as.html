What a day counts as

A day counts when you write something and give it an emoji. One entry, one day, for that plant.

Writing twice in one day does not count twice. This is not a machine to feed; it is a record of the days you actually stopped and noticed something.

Days are per plant. If you write with a coffee cup on Monday and a heart on Tuesday, each of them has one day, not two.

A day is the calendar day on your device. Writing at one in the morning counts for that morning's date.

Missing a day takes nothing away. There is no streak to break. A plant with nine days that you have not touched since spring still has nine days, and the day you write again is its tenth.

A gift brings its days with it. When somebody gives you a Gratus that took them twenty days, those twenty days arrive with it.
