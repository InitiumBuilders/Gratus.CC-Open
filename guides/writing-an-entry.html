Writing an entry

The box is on the Grow screen. Write Today on the home screen opens it with the cursor already in it.

Text. Type it. There is no length to hit.

The emoji. Tap one from the row, or Show every emoji for the full set. The emoji is what the day feeds.

Tags. Optional. Tap + Tags to open them.

Photo and Voice. A photo stays on your device. A recording is sent once to be turned into words, and the recording itself stays on your device.

Plant My Gratus saves it. One card answers: a seed for every day you have kept, and the count. On the day a plant crosses into a new phase, the card names the phase.

The time. Each entry keeps the hour it was written and how long it took.

Deleting. Open an entry from the journal and choose Delete. Its day stays on the plant.

Keeping one close. Open an entry and choose Keep Close, and it is easy to find again.
