The Return

When you give a Gratus gift, you usually never learn whether it arrived. The Return is the smallest possible answer to that.

Two moments. The person who received it can tell you that it landed, and later that it grew. Each is one tap.

What travels. The kind of event, the phase, the emoji, and the day. That is all.

What never travels. No name, no words, no time of day. Their name appears on your screen because your phone already knew who you sent it to. It was never sent anywhere.

It is their choice, every time. Neither tap is required and neither is prompted twice.

Why so little. Anything more turns a gift into a tracked package. The point is to know that it reached somebody, not to watch them.
