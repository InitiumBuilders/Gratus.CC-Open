What leaves your device

Your journal stays on your device. Entries, photos, recordings, and the time each entry was written live in your browser's own storage.

These travel, each only because you chose it:

  • A gift. Everything is inside the link: the emoji, its days, your message, and one line from your journal for each phase it passed. You decide who gets it.
  • A seed. Words written to a project you gave to. Public only if you publish them.
  • A goal. One line on a public stream. The app says so before you post.
  • A word in a room. Read by whoever holds the room's code.
  • An account, if you make one. A copy of your garden, encrypted on your device before it is sent. Nobody else can read it, and a lost password cannot be reset.

What the server keeps. Seeds, room posts, goals, account records and counters. Counters are keyed by a salted hash of an address, never an address.

Voice sends one recording at the moment you make it, to be turned into text. It is not kept there.

Clearing your browser's storage erases the garden. Export from the You sheet for a copy you keep.
