What leaves your device

Your journal never leaves. Entries, photos and recordings live in your browser's own storage on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no copy on a server.

Four things travel, each only because you chose it:

  • A gift. Everything is inside the link. You decide who gets it.
  • A seed. Words written to a project you gave to. Held until you publish them.
  • A goal. One line on a public stream. The app says so before you post.
  • A word in a room. Read by whoever holds the room's code.

What the server keeps. Seeds, room posts, goals and the counters that stop one person flooding a room. Counters are keyed by a salted hash of an address, never an address.

Voice sends one recording at the moment you make it, to be turned into text.

Clearing your browser's storage erases the garden. Export from the You sheet for a copy you keep.
