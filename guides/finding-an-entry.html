Finding an entry

The journal opens on your entries, newest first, a day at a time.

Search. Type in the box above the entries. It looks through the words, the tags and the emoji.

Filter. The button beside the search opens three more ways to narrow the list: by emoji, by tag and by folder. Press it again to close them and see everything.

Folders are yours to name: Mornings, Work, The Move. Make one with + Folder under Filter. Open an entry and tap a folder to file it there. An entry sits in one folder at a time.

Keeping one close. Open an entry and choose Keep Close. Entries kept close get their own filter.

By day. The Time Log has the calendar. Tap a lit day to read what you wrote on it.

Milestones is the third tab. It shows how many days you have kept, and the marks that have lit along the way.
