Folders, threads and milestones

The journal has four ways to look at the same entries.

Entries is everything, newest first.

Threads are entries you continued. Open an entry and choose Continue this thread; the new one is joined to it. A thread is how a single thing that kept going reads as one piece rather than as scattered days.

Folders are yours to name. Mornings, Work, The Move. An entry can sit in one folder. Make a folder from any entry with new folder.

Milestones are the days the app noticed: a phase crossed, a recipe made, a gift given, a seed bloomed. You do not create them; they accumulate.

The month. The calendar shows which days you wrote. Tap a day to read it. Hold a day to open it in full.
