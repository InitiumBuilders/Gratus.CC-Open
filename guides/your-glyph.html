Your Glyph

The Glyph is a figure drawn from your garden and nothing else.

Where. At the bottom of your garden.

How it is drawn. One ring for the garden, and one arm for each thing growing. An arm's direction comes from the emoji, so the same gratitude always points the same way. Its length is the days you gave it. Its weight is the phase. A thing that is ready to give carries a head on the end of its arm.

It is the same every time. The drawing reads nothing but your garden: no clock, no dice. Open it today and next year and it is the same figure, grown.

It is only yours. Two gardens do not draw the same figure by accident.

It never shortens, because nothing here decays. The figure only ever reaches further.
