The five phases

Every growing thing moves through five phases, by days:

phaseat
Plantedday 0
Nurturedday 2
Deepenedday 5
Bloomedday 9
Ready to giveday 13

Crossing into a phase is the only time the app makes a sound: five phases are five notes of one chord, and each crossing plays the chord as far as you have come. The fifth one falls from the top and leaves the root ringing, which is what giving sounds like.

Ready to give is not finished. A plant can stay in your garden at thirty days or three hundred. Ready means only that it now carries enough to be worth handing to somebody.

You can give something earlier than thirteen days. The app will let you. It carries fewer days, and it is still a gift.
