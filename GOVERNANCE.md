# Governance

## Who decides

August decides. This is his product and his voice, and the final call on what it says, what
it does and what it refuses to do is his.

## What that means in practice

- **Wording.** Copy marked `owner` in a config file, and everything in
  `config/his-words.json`, is his and is never edited by anybody else. Placeholder wording
  is fair game.
- **Product shape.** The twelve laws, the five growth phases, the doorway ritual and the
  refusal to rank anybody are decisions, not defaults. Changing one is a conversation, not
  a pull request.
- **Code.** Anything that does not touch the two above is an ordinary contribution: open a
  pull request, make the gates green, add the mutant.

## How a decision gets recorded

`DECISIONS.md` holds the ones that shaped the product, with the reason. If you are changing
something a decision covers, say so in the pull request and the decision gets amended
rather than quietly contradicted.
