Privacy

Updated November 20, 2026. This page describes the app as it works today.

What stays on your device

Your gratitude entries, photos, voice recordings and tags, the time each entry was written and how long it took, your emoji garden, your recipes, your wishes and your name are stored in your browser on your device. If you clear the browser's site data, or use “Start over” in the app, they are gone. “Export everything” saves a copy as a file you keep. Voice recordings are not in that file.

What travels when you choose

A Gratus Gift is packed inside the link you share. The link carries the emoji, the days it took, the phases it passed through and the dates, one short line from an entry on each of those dates, the count of your entries about that emoji, your tags, the message you wrote, who it is for, and the name you signed. Anyone who has the link can open it. Choose what you put in a gift with that in mind. The person who receives it can tell you that it arrived or that it grew. That message carries a phase, the emoji and the day, with no name and no words.

A goal you post goes to a public stream: one line, the name you gave, an emoji and the time. A word you post in a Vibe is kept on our server and can be read by anyone who holds that room's code. A Gratus Seed is a note written to a Giveth project you gave to. The people who run that project can read it, and it is public only if you publish it.

A voice entry sends the recording once to ElevenLabs to be turned into words. The recording is not kept on our server. It stays on your device.

Accounts

An account is optional. It keeps a copy of your garden that is encrypted on your device with a key made from your password, so we cannot read it. For the same reason we cannot reset a password. We keep a salted hash of your email and never the email itself. If you publish a page, it shows your handle, your name, a line you wrote and the shape of your garden. It never shows an entry. The list of people you keep is stored with your account.

Paying

If you subscribe, Stripe takes the payment on its own page and no card number reaches our server. We keep whether your subscription is active.

What we count

We count visits, the seconds a page was open, gifts wrapped, gifts planted, seeds planted and days of care, as totals. A device is counted once a day by a salted hash that is never an address. The limits on voice entries, new rooms and sign-in attempts use the same kind of hash. There are no ads and no third-party trackers.

Other services

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your browser's request for the font files. The site and its storage run on Vercel. Pages about Giveth read Giveth's public API, and a public wallet address you paste for Sunlight is sent there to be read. Links to partners open their sites, which have their own policies.
